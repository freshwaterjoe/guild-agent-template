# Research

Your research and planning notes: what you found reading an API's documentation, what a data source can and can't tell you, what others deliver, how you'd plan the method. Claude Code keeps notes here when you research with it, and drafts your agent from them.

This folder is committed to git, so your notes last, but it's never part of your agent: Guild only ever receives the manifest, `intake.md`, `specialist/` and `test-briefs/` (and, for a Guild Pro agent, its image).

Only keep here what you'd be happy to have in your repository. Never put keys or client details in it.
